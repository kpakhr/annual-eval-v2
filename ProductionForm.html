Annual Evaluation Performance Evaluation - Production/Technical

Kuwait Packing Materials Manufacturing Company (K-PAK)
DEFINITION OF RATINGS
(5) EXCEPTIONAL: Consistently exceeds all relevant performance standards.
(4) EXCEEDS EXPECTATIONS: Consistently meets and often exceeds all relevant performance standards.
(3) MEETS EXPECTATIONS: Meets all relevant performance standards.
(2) BELOW EXPECTATIONS: Sometimes meets the performance standards.
(1) NEEDS IMPROVEMENT: Consistently falls short of performance standards.
Describe the employee's contributions in each of the performance categories below. It is COMPULSORY that you illustrate specific, detailed examples. Ratings MUST be supported and substantiated by narrative comments.
I. TECHNICAL / WORKMANSHIP
Competency
I.1 PRODUCTIVITY (VOLUME & QUALITY OF WORK) *
a.) Maintains steady, acceptable level of work output.
b.) Quality work adheres to standards and procedures. Keeps complete and reliable records of work.
c.) Maintains acceptable standards of workmanship.
d.) Indicate the average productivity efficiency of the employee.
I.2 JOB KNOWLEDGE *
a.) Understanding of job procedures, equipment and methods, responsibilities and scope of duties.
b.) Competence in acquiring and analyzing the nature of work assignment and its corresponding action to be taken.
I.3 COOPERATION (Teamwork & conflict resolution) *
Works with others to accomplish the goals of the job and work group. Demonstrates ability to get along with others, is respectful of co-workers, communicates and acts as a team player, promotes teamwork, responds and acts appropriately to confrontational situations.
II. BEHAVIORAL ATTRIBUTES
Competency
II.1 WORK SAFETY AND MAINTENANCE OF RESOURCES *
Organized and neat. Ensures safety of self and others through proper use and care of equipment/work site. Handles and operates equipment in a careful manner; keeps tools and equipment in working order and stores them properly. Keeps the work site clear of potential hazards. Employee maintains a safe work environment and ensures equipment is operating properly.
II.2 FLEXIBILITY AND WORK UNDER PRESSURE *
Demonstrates adaptability and flexibility to work on different shifts, schedules or departments. Accepts changes on their daily work. Strives to learn and improve. Seeks out ways to better himself. Takes on responsibilities. Does not remain idle.
II.3 COMMITMENT TO JOB *
a.) Demonstrates a consistent, dependable work effort and positive work attitude.
b.) Responds positively in taking new assignments and accepts challenge so that work can be completed. Takes on additional responsibilities readily. Adaptable to changes in work assignments.
